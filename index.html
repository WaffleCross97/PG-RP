How ModelApplier Works:

  • Custom Model Name: Your 3D model file name (goes in models/item/)
  • Item Name: What users rename items to in-game (goes in items/)
  • Three files are created:
    1. items/{item_name}.json ← Tells ModelApplier what model to use
    2. models/item/{model_name}.json ← Your 3D model
    3. textures/item/{model_name}.png ← Your texture
  • In-game usage:
    1. Rename any item to your "Item Name"
    2. ModelApplier looks for items/{item_name}.json
    3. Finds "model": "minecraft:item/{model_name}"
    4. Uses your custom model from models/item/{model_name}.json
